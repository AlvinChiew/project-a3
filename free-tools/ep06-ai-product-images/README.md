# AI Product Images — User Guide

Simple steps for non-technical sellers who want clean product photos for Shopee, RedNote, or other marketplaces.

## Before you start

1. Get a free activation code at [project-a3.alvinchiew.com](https://project-a3.alvinchiew.com/#signup).
2. Add your **OpenAI API key** in **Settings** and click **Test connection**.
3. Prepare **one clear product photo** (PNG or JPG).

## Create a project

1. Open **Projects** → **New project**.
2. Choose your product photo.
3. Fill in **product details** (name, price, offer, brand colors) and **Save**.

## White-bg hero (default)

1. Stay on **White-bg hero** mode.
2. Click **Generate white-bg hero**.
3. When the preview appears, tick **Approve for export** if you are happy.
4. Click **Export approved PNGs (ZIP)** and choose a folder.

## 9-pack listing shots (optional)

1. Switch pack mode to **9-pack listing shots**.
2. Open any **slot** to edit headline, bullets, or prompt notes — your edits are kept when you return.
3. **Generate** or **Regenerate** each slot.
4. **Approve** the shots you want.
5. **Export** the ZIP.

## Tips

- AI images can differ from your real product — always review before publishing.
- Only **approved** images are included in the ZIP.
- Re-open a slot to tweak text; defaults will not overwrite your edits.
